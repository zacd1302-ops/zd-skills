## What it does

`unslop` removes common AI writing patterns and replaces them with plain, specific prose. It checks for filler, puffery, vague claims, excessive headings, jargon, hedging, passive voice, and sentences that do not tell the reader anything concrete.

It also preserves a human voice. Shorter is not enough if the result becomes sterile or says nothing specific.

## When to reach for it

Type `/unslop`, or the agent reaches for it automatically when a task fits. Reach for it when cleaning prose, reviewing a document, or writing any agent-facing instruction.

## The rewrite test

Ask what the reader needs to know or do. State that directly, use the project's real names, split dense sentences, and remove words that do no work. Avoid em dashes and decorative formatting.

## It's working if

- The text makes concrete claims instead of describing how something feels.
- Each sentence has one clear job and uses plain words.
- The result sounds like a person with a point of view, not a generic assistant.
